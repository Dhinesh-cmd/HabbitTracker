📊 Clear History & Analytics?

This will permanently delete all stored history, analytics, charts, reports, and statistics.

Your habits, settings, categories, and login information will remain intact.

This action cannot be undone.

🗑️

Clear All Habits?

Are you sure you want to permanently clear all habits and their associated completion records?

This will permanently remove:

  • ✕ All habits across Diet, Routine, Skin Care, Hair Care & Others
  • ✕ Completion history & timestamps for all habits
  • ✕ Habit-dependent streaks and leaderboard rankings

This will preserve:

  • ✓ User profile (name, age, login session)
  • ✓ Sleep time, sound alerts, and theme preferences
  • ✓ Non-habit daily logs (water, sleep, notes)

This action cannot be undone.
